30 motion effects that make a site feel designed

Each one is a single HTML file you can get from an AI with one prompt. Watch it play, copy its prompt, swap in your own words and colours.

The format is inspired by @zeeuiux's “50 Claude animations”. These effects are our own designs, built and tested by us. A few (How It Works, Marquee, Char Rise, Proof Badge, CTA Wall) copy only the TIMING of animations on the public horizonx.so homepage, measured and rebuilt with our own design and words. Not sponsored.

How to use them

  1. Copy a prompt (each one is complete on its own).
  2. Paste it into Claude (claude.ai or Claude Code) or another AI that writes code, and ask for one HTML file.
  3. Open the file in your browser. It plays and loops.
  4. Make it yours: change the line in [brackets] and the Brand part (colours and fonts).

Expect a close match, not a copy: every run comes out a little different.
